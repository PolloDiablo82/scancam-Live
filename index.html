Scan Cam Live

The mat gives the exact size and steady alignment, even on plain leather. Print the mat (A4), put the subject in the middle circle, the mat on the stand so it turns with it.

Shoot in shade or even light. When you're done, tap Send to Scan Lab and pick LocalSend, device Laptop Scan Lab. The laptop builds the model by itself.

Each room: Loop 1 and Loop 2 (big or L-shaped rooms also Middle), then send it. Then film a doorway walk from a finished room into the next one, so the laptop can join the rooms into one house.
