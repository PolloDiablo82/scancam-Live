Scan Cam Live

Shoot in shade or even light. When you're done, tap Send to Scan Lab and pick LocalSend, device Laptop Scan Lab. The laptop builds the model by itself.

Extra close-ups

Close-ups of a part the walk-around misses: thin, shiny or hidden parts. Name the part, then take photos (or a short video) moving around it, half a step between shots, the part filling about half the screen. Keep a bit of the rest of the object in view so the laptop can place them.
